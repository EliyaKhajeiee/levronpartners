## Key results

- Daily admin dropped from **3–4 hrs to under 1 hr**
- **50–60 hours a month** recovered and redirected to additional projects
- Calls, texts, and spreadsheets replaced by one centralized system
- Client updates now automated — no manual communication required
- Team capacity increased without adding headcount

## The problem

South Central Investment Group was managing job scheduling, client communication, and project updates across calls, texts, and spreadsheets. There was no central system — just a collection of manual touchpoints that required constant attention.

The owner was spending 3–4 hours every day coordinating: confirming schedules, updating clients, checking project status, answering the same questions repeatedly because there was no automated way for clients or the team to get answers themselves.

At that pace, taking on additional projects meant adding more hours to an already full day — the classic constraint every growing GC hits before they realize it's a systems problem, not a headcount problem.

## What we found

The audit found a clear pattern: every coordination task was manual by default. Nothing was routing information or updating stakeholders on its own. The workflows themselves were simple and repeatable — exactly the kind that should run without a person in the loop, but invisible as a fixable cost until someone actually mapped the day.

## What we built

A centralized job management and communication system designed around how a general contracting operation actually runs:

- Centralized scheduling across all active jobs
- Automated client update notifications at key project milestones
- Real-time project status tracking for the whole team
- Communication history logged per job
- Automated reminders reducing missed appointments and delays

The owner stopped being the communication layer between the team and the client.

## The result

| Metric | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Daily coordination time | 3–4 hrs | Under 1 hr |
| Client updates | Manual calls/texts | Automated |
| Project visibility | Spreadsheets | Centralized system |
| Job capacity | Capped | Increased |

**50–60 hours recovered every month** — worth roughly **$49,500/year** at a conservative $75/hr operator rate.

> "It gave me my day back."
> — **Shahriar Shabazi**, Owner, South Central Investment Group

## What this means if you run a GC or design-build firm

If you're winning enough work that estimating and coordination are becoming the constraint — not the tools, not the crew — this is the pattern to look for: manual scheduling, manual client updates, and status living in someone's head instead of a system. Fixing that doesn't require new software so much as connecting what you already have.
